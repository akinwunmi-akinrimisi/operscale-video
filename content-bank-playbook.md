# Content Bank Playbook

> The 90-video pre-launch content bank that fuels organic distribution and acts as our case-study library.

**Read alongside:** `content-bank-30-day-plan.html`, `customer-journey.md`.

---

## What the content bank is

90 short-form videos produced before public launch. 30 days × 3 posts/day, distributed across our 5 target niches. The content bank serves three purposes:

1. **Organic distribution.** Posted to our own Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn pages, the bank seeds an audience and demonstrates capability before we spend on paid ads.
2. **Case-study library.** Customers visiting `plovera.shop` see a portfolio of work. The case-study pages at `/o/<order_id>` (anonymous) feature these.
3. **Internal QA training set.** 90 videos across 5 niches gives the founder a depth of reference for what "good" looks like in each niche before paying customers arrive.

---

## Distribution

5 niches × 6 videos per niche × 3 weeks = 90 videos. Per niche per week:

- 3 documentary-style videos (the default register)
- 1 testimonial-style "as if customer" video
- 1 controversy/hook-led video that probes engagement

For details on the per-week posting cadence, see `content-bank-30-day-plan.html`.

## Production specs

Every content-bank video is rendered through the same forked Vision GridAI pipeline that our paying customers use. Same TTS, same image generation, same captions. This is deliberate — the content bank IS our QA validation that the pipeline works.

**Voice:** Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD across all niches. The default voice is `en-NG-Standard-A` (Nigerian English). For variety, niches may use:
- Real estate: `en-NG-Standard-A` (warm, authoritative)
- Education: `en-NG-Standard-A` or `en-NG-Standard-B` (alternates for variety)
- Fashion/beauty: `en-NG-Standard-A` (slightly faster speaking rate, 1.0× → 1.05×)
- Fintech: `en-NG-Standard-A` (clinical, 1.0×)
- Health: `en-NG-Standard-A` (calm, 0.95×)

We do NOT use ElevenLabs for content bank or paid orders (per ADR 0009). Voice cloning via fal.ai PlayHT v3 is reserved for Creative Pod paying customers (ADR 0010).

**Captions:** Niche-styled with niche-specific emphasis colour, matching our paid Standard tier:
- Real estate: gold emphasis on `caption_highlight_word`
- Education: indigo emphasis
- Fashion/beauty: red emphasis (the default)
- Fintech: cyan emphasis with JetBrains Mono font
- Health: sage emphasis

Generated by the inherited `generate_kinetic_ass.py` with the niche parameter — see `docs/VISION_GRIDAI_FORK_MANUAL.md` §8.7.

**Visual mix:** All static images + Ken Burns motion (matches our Pilot tier visual mix). No I2V, no avatar-led for content bank — these features are paid-tier only and we want the content bank to demonstrate the floor of what we deliver, not the ceiling.

**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 only (1080×1920). Posted natively to TikTok/Reels/Shorts with separate captions per platform.

**Length:** 15-30 seconds, matching Pilot tier. Hook in first 3 seconds, payoff by second 25.

**Music:** Royalty-free curated library. Same as paid Pilot tier.

## Topic generation for the content bank

Unlike paid orders, content bank topics aren't customer-driven. They're generated weekly by founder + Claude Opus 4.7:

1. Founder picks 5 themes for the week (one per niche)
2. Each theme generates 18 topic candidates via Claude Opus
3. Founder picks 6 winners per niche (30 videos total per week)
4. Topics flow into the same `briefs` → `orders` pipeline as paid orders, with a `source = 'content_bank'` flag

Topics for each niche are heavily informed by the niche briefs in `niche-briefs/`. Each topic produces a script that fits the niche's tone, angles, and content rules.

## What the content bank teaches us before paying customers arrive

Each batch of 30 videos (one week) reveals:
- Which niche tones land best (engagement metrics on IG/TikTok)
- Which captions get screenshotted (high-emphasis-word frequency)
- Which CTAs get clicked (we link to `plovera.shop` from each post)
- Where the pipeline has wrinkles (e.g., consistent fal.ai rate-limit hits during 9-11pm renders)

By the end of week 3 (90 videos), we've stress-tested the pipeline at sustained ~13 videos/day rate. If we can do this without paying customers in the queue, we know we can handle launch traffic.

## Cross-platform optimisation

Each video is rendered once at 9:16 1080×1920 and posted natively (uploaded to each platform separately, not cross-posted). This avoids:
- TikTok's watermark on Reels
- Algorithm penalties for "this looks like a TikTok video" on Instagram

The native upload means each video gets a unique caption per platform:
- Instagram: longer caption with 3 hashtags
- TikTok: shorter caption with 5 hashtags
- LinkedIn (selected longer-form posts only): full paragraph context

---

## Quality bar

A content-bank video ships if:
- Captions sync within 100ms (Whisper alignment confidence ≥ 0.85)
- Audio loudnorm passes (-16 LUFS target)
- Image consistency: no face artefacts, no garbled text in image
- Caption text accuracy: matches script narration verbatim
- Length within 5% of intended duration

A video that fails any of these gets regenerated. Better to ship 25 polished videos than 30 sloppy ones.

---

## Content bank → paid pipeline transition

Once paid orders begin (Phase 1 of launch), the content bank slows from 13 videos/day to 5-7 videos/day. Founder review time shifts toward paid orders. The bank continues at lower velocity — we keep posting throughout the year, not just pre-launch.

Successful content-bank posts can be re-rendered as paid-tier examples for new customers in that niche ("here's what works for fashion in Lagos"). This is a free upsell tool.

## Costs

Per video: ~$1.50 ($1 LLM, $0.30 TTS, $0.20 images). 90 videos × $1.50 = ~$135 plus $20-50/month VPS marginal. Total content bank investment: ~$155-185.

Time investment: founder ~30 hours over 3 weeks (topic curation + Gate 0/2/3 reviews). Engineer time: zero — this is the founder's tool to validate the pipeline before launch.
